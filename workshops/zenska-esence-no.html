Female Essence and Life Force

A holistic journey through the four seasons of the year

Over the course of the year, we meet for four sacred gatherings dedicated to profound rest and self-care. Not superficial relaxation, but deep restoration for body, mind, and inner alignment.

A sacred space where you have nothing to prove, nowhere to rush, and nothing to manage. Here you are welcomed simply to be, held by a warm and supportive circle of women.

What awaits you in the circle?

  • Gentle somatic bodywork, breathwork, and intuitive movement
  • Deep restorative rest and meditation aligned with natural cycles
  • Nourishing, lovingly prepared food to support recovery and wellness
  • Exploring sacred sisterhood, authentic vulnerability, and shared wisdom
  • Awakening your womb energy, innate intuition, and cyclical power

Who is this workshop for?

  • Women in need of real rest, stillness, and restorative sanctuary
  • Those wanting to align their lifestyle with the seasons and female cycles
  • Anyone seeking an empowering, non-judgmental community of women

Pusteritual for heling av «søstersåret»

Et dypt forankret, intimt pusteritual som har som mål å hele søstersåret og gjenoppbygge tillit, trygghet og kontakt mellom kvinner.

Gjennom sammenhengende pust, somatisk oppmerksomhet og emosjonelt uttrykk møter og forløser vi skånsomt følelser lagret i kroppen. Søstersår – ofte formet av sammenligning, fordømmelse, utestengelse eller svik – sitter ikke bare i minnet, men i nervesystemet.

Pusten bidrar til å myke opp forsvarsmønstre, frigjøre fastlåst emosjonell energi og åpne opp for ekte tilknytning og levende tillit.
