Personal Consultations

I offer personal consultations for both couples and individuals. I will help you discover solutions to your challenges. I will not solve them for you; I will accompany you to your desired destination. The most meaningful part of the work is yours to unfold.

I offer consultations in person or online via Skype. If you are interested, please reach out through the contact form.

  • Resolving relationship challenges across all intimacy areas, see the 6 Pillars of Intimacy.

  • Low or diminished sexual desire.

  • De-stigmatizing sensitive intimacy topics.
  • Erectile dysfunction challenges.
  • Premature ejaculation.
  • Communication blocks between partners.
  • Self-love and reconnecting with your own body.
