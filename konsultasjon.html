Personal Consultations

When you seek private one-on-one or couples guidance in a trusted, confidential space. Some conversations simply deserve to happen face to face. I am here for you.

Private Sessions

I offer specialized personal consultations for both couples and individuals. I partner with you to discover authentic resolutions to intimate struggles. I do not "fix" problems for you; rather, I hold space and gently walk beside you to your chosen destination. The true transformative journey is yours to claim.

How Do We Meet?

Sessions are held either in person or worldwide via online video (Skype / Zoom). Please write to me through the contact form below to coordinate our first session.

Areas of Focus & Support
  • Resolving partnership impasses across all dimensions of intimacy (refer to the 6 Pillars of Intimacy).
  • Pain or physical discomfort during sexual intercourse.
  • Hypoactive or mismatched libido / loss of sexual desire.
  • Post-operative rehabilitation – cesarean section recovery, episiotomy scar tissue release, and somatic pelvic healing.
  • Emotional and sexual trauma integration.
  • De-tabooing vulnerable intimate desires and honest communication.
  • Erectile dysfunction and performance stress.
  • Premature or delayed ejaculation challenges.
  • Self-acceptance, body dysmorphia, and cultivating embodied self-love.

Suitable For

Adult men, women, and couples seeking respectful somatic and emotional growth.
