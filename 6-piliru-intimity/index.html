6 Pillars of Intimacy

Intimacy is that comforting feeling of safety, merge, and deep trust between people. It does not appear by chance; it requires care, presence, and dedication. It accompanies us throughout every stage of our life.

From my perspective, intimacy rests upon 6 foundational pillars. In consultations with clients, we focus primarily on the relationship with our own body. Everything begins and ends with self-love. When I love myself, I can genuinely love others. When I cultivate self-compassion, I can extend it to others in all seasons of life.

Respect, love, and compassion.

1. Couple Intimacy & Relationship with One's Own Body

Often we simply struggle to understand ourselves—and each other. Yet we all yearn for the same thing: to love and be loved, and to genuinely enjoy our romantic partnership and sexuality. We can only fully accept someone else once we fully embrace ourselves. Loving our own body is often the greatest hurdle, meaning accepting it precisely as it is. I work with couples both individually and together.

2. Intimacy in Pregnancy & Birth

You love your body as it is; you have grown familiar with it and take good care of it. Suddenly, pregnancy initiates profound, uncontrollable shifts. It changes to the point where you might barely recognize yourself, responding differently to touch, movement, and affection. Nurturing intimacy as a couple throughout pregnancy is frequently a sensitive challenge. I am here to guide you safely through this profound chapter.

Book a Consultation

3. Intimacy in Motherhood & Family Bonds

Your long-awaited child has arrived. You are joyful parents, yet your daily reality has turned upside down. Romantic intimacy often takes a sudden backseat. How do you rekindle that connection? How do you navigate this brand-new dynamic as partners? Having walked this path myself, I know what truly supports reconnection—all it takes is the mutual will to begin.

Book a Consultation

4. Intimacy in Children & Adolescents

During the first 18 months, children already begin forming an intimate relationship with their physical selves. Do you feel anxious about early questions regarding bodies and sexuality? Are you unsure how to respond when your little one explores private parts? Parents hold tremendous influence in cultivating a healthy body image and setting the stage for respectful future relationships. Together, we address your worries and prepare you for insightful, open conversations.

Book a Consultation

5. Intimacy in Mature Age

If you have shared a long-term bond, you might believe there is nothing left to learn about each other. Let me prove otherwise. You will be amazed at the renewed passion you can discover and how you can fall in love all over again, even after decades together. And if you are single following a divorce or long separation, we will explore your inner strengths and show you how richly intimacy can flourish at any age.

Book a Consultation

6. Intimacy in Dying & Departure

Everything in life eventually runs its course. It rests within our hands whether we are able to accompany our loved ones through their final journey with reverence, peace, and deep human presence.
