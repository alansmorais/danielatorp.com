Who is Daniela

I am human. I feel, perceive, and hurt just like you. I experience equivalent crossroads, struggles, joys, and heartaches. I nurture myself, my family, and my community. I revere the natural world, bowing humbly before its laws, its rich biodiversity, and its playful humor.

Did you know there are more than 180,000 islands on Earth?

Countless islands appear identical from afar, yet each nurtures an irreplaceable, self-contained ecosystem.

Can you imagine forcing plum trees to fruit amidst coconut palms? You might coax a bitter harvest through exhaustion, but you are fighting nature.

We routinely subject ourselves to the exact same violence—compelling our bodies and spirits to satisfy external molds.

I explored thousands of islands before finding my own shores.

The sovereign key to fulfillment is love: beginning unconditionally with yourself.
