Who is Daniela

I am a human being. I feel and perceive just like you. I experience similar situations, challenges, joys, and sorrows. I care for myself, my family, and my friends. I honor nature and bow humbly before its laws, its infinite diversity, and its sense of humor.

Did you know that there are more than 180,000 islands in the world?

There are thousands of islands, and they often look very similar. Yet each one is a unique, sovereign ecosystem.

Can you imagine trying to grow plum trees in a paradise of coconut palms? It might work for a while, but you will exhaust yourself trying to force it to succeed.

We often treat ourselves the exact same way. We force ourselves to do things that conflict with our true essence.

I personally journeyed through thousands of islands before finding my own.

The key to success is love—love for yourself.
