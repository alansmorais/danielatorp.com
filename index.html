Who is Daniela

I am human. I feel and perceive just like you. I experience similar situations, challenges, joys, and sorrows. I care for myself, my family, and my friends. I honor nature and humbly bow before its laws, its endless diversity, and its sense of humor.

Did you know there are more than 180,000 islands in the world?

There are thousands of islands, and they often look alike. Yet each is a unique ecosystem.

Can you imagine trying to grow plums in a paradise of coconut palms? It might work for a while, but you must exert tremendous effort to succeed.

Often we treat ourselves the same way. We force ourselves to do things that conflict with our true essence.

I have walked through thousands of islands myself before discovering my own.

The key to fulfillment is love—love for yourself.
